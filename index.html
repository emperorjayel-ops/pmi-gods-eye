The God's Eye scholarship is how students get in on it: apply here, take the test, and the award helps carry you through the program.

The Academy & Certification

Through the Academy, people get certified — and once certified, they can certify others. Certification is a chain: each person who earns it can bring the next person up to the same standard.

Scholarship recipients enter that chain through the Academy program tied to the school being built.

The Excalibur Test

The way in is the God's Eye test — the Excalibur test. The test is about the device: scholarships worth $20,000 are awarded to people who can activate the device with their left hand or their right hand.

The device doesn't work for everybody — that's the test. If you can activate it, left or right, you qualify for the award.

How it works

  1. Apply on this app — fill in the application below. It takes two minutes.
  2. Pay the $50 application fee in Bitcoin — the fee is the price of taking the God's Eye test. It is paid in Bitcoin through FinSwap / Bitcoin, the same way Jay's other apps handle Bitcoin payments (demo-labeled below until a live payment integration is in place).
  3. Take the Excalibur test — activate the device with your left hand or your right hand. Detailed test instructions are being sent by Jay and will be posted in the space marked below — the application flow is ready for them now.
  4. The $20,000 scholarship — awarded to applicants who can activate the device. Awards are decided by administration in the Back Office, where every application lands.
Detailed Excalibur test information — coming from Jay: the full test instructions (device, procedure, scheduling) will be filled in here exactly as Jay sends them. Nothing about the test beyond what is stated above has been invented or filled in.

GitHub & Open Source

What was pulled, what powers this

Jay asked for God's Eye to be pulled from GitHub and rebuilt with proven open-source code so it is interactive instead of static. Here is exactly what that means for this build — checked against the repos, not guessed.

Pulled from Jay's GitHub — Gods-Eye-2

github.com/emperorjayel-ops/Gods-Eye-2

Jay's own repo (checked 2026-11-23). What is in it today: the HIIYAH eSIM app codebase (hiiyah-esim-app/ — a store-ready telecom app built on open-source eSIM code), the repo README, and the Broker credit file. That HIIYAH codebase is why HIIYAH is the talk layer in this app: the repo Jay calls “the God's Eye situation” carries the family's telecom inside it.

The repo's own honesty note travels with it: an app alone does not provision real eSIM profiles — that takes a carrier / white-label partner with a GSMA-certified SM-DP+ server. Same rule is honored in the HIIYAH section above.
