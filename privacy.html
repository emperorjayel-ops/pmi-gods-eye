About this app

PMI God's Eye is an interactive app by OMNI-PRESENT SOLUTIONS, INC.: a World View map, a video library where members can add and play videos, member-to-admin messaging through HIIYAH, scholarship program information, and the God's Eye (Excalibur) test, with a $20,000 scholarship for qualifiers.

What data the app handles

  • Member videos: videos a member adds are kept for that app session only and are not uploaded to or stored on our servers by this app.
  • Member and admin accounts: the member and administrator doors in this app are demo accounts printed in the app; activity is session-only on your device.
  • Camera preview: any camera preview in the app is local to your device only. It is not recorded, transmitted, or stored.
  • Messaging: member-to-admin talk through HIIYAH is a demo in this app; no live calling is connected yet.
  • Map data: the World View loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap; that provider receives the technical requests needed to display the map, under its own privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data to anyone.
  • We do not run third-party advertising networks or ad trackers in this app.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, call logs, or device identifiers for advertising.

Payments and financial features

The $50 Bitcoin fee to take the God's Eye test is shown in the app as a demo fee slip routed through FinSwap. No live payment connection exists in this app and no money is collected through it at this time.

Data retention

Session-only data (videos, messages, account activity) is cleared when the session ends and is not retained on our servers.

Children

This app is directed to users aged 13 and over. Users under 13 are not a target audience for this app.
